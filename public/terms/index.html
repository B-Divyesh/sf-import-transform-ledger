The service

Import Transform Ledger is a local-first CSV mapping, cleanup, validation, and audit utility. It does not connect to destination business systems, infer sensitive mappings, or submit imports on your behalf.

Free workflow and Field Kit

The full transform, review, rejection, recipe export, output export, and checksum workflow is available without payment. Field Kit is a $29 one-time license that unlocks an unlimited saved recipe library on the licensed device when purchases are available. It is not a subscription. Accessibility, data export, and rejection safety are never paywalled.

When enabled, purchases use Sociobot’s hosted billing system, with Dodo as merchant of record. Refunds are handled by the merchant of record; a refunded, revoked, expired, invalid, or wrong-product license stops unlocking paid features. An enabled purchase may be restored on another device by pasting its token. The product clearly states when purchases and restoration are not open.
