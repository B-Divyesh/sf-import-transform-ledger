Field policy 01

Privacy, without fine print.

Your CSV content stays in your browser. We do not receive, inspect, or upload source files, target templates, transformed rows, rejection ledgers, or saved recipes.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the active workspace, saved recipes, and local run reports in IndexedDB so work survives a refresh. When Field Kit purchases and restoration are enabled, a license token, verification timing, and the last verdict are stored in localStorage. Closed-purchase builds do not accept or store license tokens. You can clear local data through your browser’s site-data controls. Exported files go only to the location your browser chooses.

Data sent over the network

The installed app shell is served from import-transform-ledger.sociobot.in. When Field Kit purchases and restoration are enabled, buying or verifying a license connects your browser to the Sociobot billing API and sends only the license token for verification. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot; Dodo is the merchant of record and handles payment data under its own terms. CSV data is never included in those requests. Closed-purchase builds make no billing API request.

Analytics and tracking

This version includes no analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party runtime scripts.

Offline storage and service worker

A service worker caches application files, artwork, and legal pages so the utility works offline after the first successful visit. It does not cache CSV uploads or exported files. Uninstall the app or clear site data to remove cached files.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests about server-side purchase records can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in. Local workspace data is controlled and deleted by you through your browser.
